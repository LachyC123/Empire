This is separate from The Turning.

The core requirement: I walk around a physical operation. If I hire a packer, a real new worker arrives at an available packaging position and performs the job. If I upgrade speed, the work visibly and mechanically speeds up. Better equipment changes its sprite, animation and output. Upgrading enough unlocks new stations, products, rooms and activities. Make it easy: clear Hire/Upgrade/Build/Craft buttons with exact cost and useful next benefit, no complicated menus or dozens of unclear stats.

Start with M0–M2.
